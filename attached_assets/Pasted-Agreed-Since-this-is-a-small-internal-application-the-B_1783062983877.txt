# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Planning Department Interactive Platform

**Consumer Protection Authority (CPA)**

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Project Overview

The Consumer Protection Authority (CPA) intends to develop a simple, responsive internal web application for the Planning Department. The platform will serve as a centralized communication and collaboration hub where employees can access planning information, submit inquiries and suggestions, participate in discussions, and view planning-related announcements.

The application should be simple, user-friendly, and accessible from desktop and mobile devices.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The objectives of the application are to:

* Improve communication between the Planning Department and employees.
* Centralize planning-related information in one location.
* Reduce email communication for planning matters.
* Encourage employee participation and engagement.
* Provide an organized channel for inquiries and suggestions.
* Improve awareness of planning initiatives and updates.

---

# 3. Scope

The system will consist of a single responsive dashboard that provides access to the following functions:

* Announcements & Updates
* Participatory Discussions
* Employee Inquiries
* Knowledge Base
* Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* Suggestions

---

# 4. Functional Requirements

## 4.1 Dashboard

The system shall provide a home page displaying all available modules in a simple dashboard layout.

---

## 4.2 Announcements & Updates

The Planning Department shall be able to:

* Publish announcements.
* Share planning updates.
* Publish circulars.
* Inform employees about important dates and deadlines.
* Archive previous announcements.

Employees shall be able to:

* View announcements.
* Search previous announcements.

---

## 4.3 Participatory Discussions

Employees shall be able to:

* Participate in planning discussions.
* Post comments.
* Reply to existing discussions.

Planning Department staff shall be able to:

* Create discussion topics.
* Moderate discussions.
* Close discussions when required.

---

## 4.4 Employee Inquiries

Employees shall be able to:

* Submit planning-related inquiries.
* Track the status of their inquiries.
* View responses from the Planning Department.

Planning Department staff shall be able to:

* View submitted inquiries.
* Respond to inquiries.
* Mark inquiries as resolved.

---

## 4.5 Knowledge Base

The system shall provide access to planning reference documents, including:

* Planning manuals.
* Operational planning guidelines.
* KPI guidelines.
* Policies.
* Templates.
* Other planning documents.

Employees shall be able to search and download available documents.

---

## 4.6 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The system shall display commonly asked planning questions and answers.

Planning Department staff shall be able to add, edit, and remove FAQ entries.

---

## 4.7 Suggestions

Employees shall be able to submit suggestions related to:

* Planning improvements.
* New initiatives.
* Process enhancements.
* General feedback.

Planning Department staff shall be able to:

* Review submitted suggestions.
* Update their status (New, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, Implemented).
* Provide feedback to employees.

---

# 5. User Roles

### Employee

* View announcements.
* Participate in discussions.
* Submit inquiries.
* Submit suggestions.
* Access the knowledge base.
* View FAQs.

### Planning Department Officer

* Manage announcements.
* Respond to inquiries.
* Moderate discussions.
* Review suggestions.
* Manage knowledge base documents.
* Maintain FAQs.

### Planning Department Manager

* Monitor platform usage.
* Review discussions.
* Oversee inquiries and suggestions.
* Approve published content.

### System Administrator

* Manage users.
* Configure permissions.
* Maintain system settings.

---

# 6. Non-Functional Requirements

The application shall:

* Be responsive and accessible on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* Support both Arabic and English.
* Integrate with CPA user authentication (Single Sign-On if available).
* Provide secure access based on user roles.
* Offer a simple and intuitive user interface.
* Ensure acceptable response times for all users.

---

# 7. Technology Recommendation

* **Platform:** Joget DX
* **Database:** PostgreSQL or Microsoft SQL Server
* **Authentication:** Microsoft Entra ID / Active Directory
* **Deployment:** CPA internal infrastructure or private cloud

---

# 8. Expected Benefits

* Centralized planning communication.
* Improved employee engagement.
* Faster dissemination of planning updates.
* Better management of employee inquiries and suggestions.
* Reduced dependency on emails.
* Easy access to planning knowledge and reference materials.

---

# 9. Future Enhancements

The following features may be considered in future phases:

* Annual and operational plan monitoring.
* KPI tracking dashboards.
* Approval workflows.
* Notifications and reminders.
* Reporting and analytics.
* Integration with Microsoft Teams and Outlook.

This version keeps the application intentionally lightweight and practical, making it suitable for rapid implementation while covering the Planning Department's essential communication and collaboration needs.
